01/Practice

We turn complex constraints into spaces that feel inevitable.

Sedes Architecture is a California practice serving homeowners, businesses, and builders throughout the Bay Area. We bring design ambition and technical discipline into the same conversation.

Every project is developed around how people live and work, how a building is assembled, and how it moves through approval. The result is architecture with purpose—calm, durable, and specific to its place.
